# Storyboard — Why Are Octopuses So Smart? (They Shouldn't Be)

## Style bible
Rich painterly digital illustration, visible soft brush texture, deep-ocean teal and midnight-blue palette with warm coral and amber accents, soft volumetric god-rays through water, gentle floating particles, cinematic 16:9 composition, shallow depth of field, wondrous and slightly mysterious mood, no text, no letters, no watermarks.

_Alternative considered:_ clean flat-vector "science explainer" look (bold shapes, limited palette). Rejected for now — painterly suits the "alien wonder" tone better.
